PROJECT MATCHPOINT

BARCADE + SOCIAL MATCHMAKING LOUNGE

Business Proposal & Investor Concept

Working concept • Location to be selected

 

A nightlife venue where people come to PLAY, CONNECT, and MEET.



1. Executive Summary
Project MatchPoint is a modern barcade built around a simple idea: turn nightlife into an interactive social experience. The venue combines a full-service bar, arcade games, competitive gaming, music, and a proprietary matchmaking tablet experience that lets guests discover compatible people in the venue without the awkwardness of approaching strangers cold.

The concept is designed to capture multiple spending occasions—date nights, singles nights, birthdays, group outings, after-work gatherings, watch parties, tournaments, and late-night social events—while creating a recurring reason for guests to return.

The Opportunity
• Differentiate from traditional bars through a technology-enabled social experience.
• Create a natural reason for guests to interact through games and matchmaking prompts.
• Generate revenue from drinks, food, games, events, premium matchmaking features, and private bookings.
• Build a highly shareable concept that can grow through short-form social content and themed events.

3. The Matchmaking Tablet System
The technology should be designed around consent, privacy and mutual opt-in. A sample guest journey:

1. Guest checks in and chooses whether to participate in MatchPoint.
2. Guest creates a lightweight profile: age range, interests, preferred vibe, relationship intent and optional photos.
3. The system displays compatible participating guests currently in the venue.
4. A guest can send a private 'Match Request' or a game challenge; contact details are not revealed automatically.
5. A match occurs only after mutual acceptance.
6. Matched guests can receive a suggested icebreaker, game recommendation or designated meetup zone.
7. Guests can pause visibility, block/report another user, or delete their profile.
Important operating principle: the system should never publicly label someone as single, available, rejected, or unmatched. Participation must be opt-in, and the venue should establish clear privacy, conduct, age-verification and data-retention policies.

9. Marketing & Customer Acquisition
• Launch campaign: 'Don't Swipe. Step Out.' Build the brand around real-world interaction.
• Weekly Singles Night: rotating themes, hosts, DJs and limited matchmaking challenges.
• Arcade Tournament Nights: bracket competitions with prizes and social content.
• Match of the Night: anonymous/consensual spotlight on a successful in-venue connection.
• Creator nights: invite local creators to test the concept and document the experience.
• Short-form video: reaction clips, first-date challenges, funny matchmaking prompts, tournament moments and customer testimonials.
• Referral program: guests receive game credits or event perks for bringing new guests.
• Birthday and corporate packages: use private events to monetize slower periods.

11. Key Risk & Compliance Considerations
• Alcohol licensing and local business requirements must be confirmed for the chosen jurisdiction before signing a final lease.
• Age verification and responsible-service procedures must be built into the operating model.
• The matchmaking platform should use clear consent, privacy, moderation, blocking/reporting and data-retention rules.
• Terms of use should explain that matches are introductions, not endorsements or guarantees of safety/compatibility.
• Cybersecurity and payment security should be designed into the tablet and POS architecture.
• Accessibility, fire/life-safety, occupancy, food-service and entertainment requirements must be reviewed with qualified local professionals.
• The venue should maintain a documented harassment policy and rapid escalation process for guest complaints.

13. Funding Request
A preliminary capital target of $750,000–$1.25 million is recommended for a professionally built first location, depending on the final size, condition of the space, equipment package and local licensing costs. The capital would fund build-out, equipment, technology, pre-opening costs, launch marketing and working capital.

Potential funding structures:

• Founder equity + outside investor equity
• SBA/commercial financing where eligible
• Strategic hospitality or entertainment investor
• Equipment financing for arcade, bar and technology components
• Hybrid structure combining investor capital and debt

15. Investment Thesis
Project MatchPoint is positioned as more than a bar and more than an arcade. It is an entertainment platform built around a human need: people want to meet, compete, laugh and connect in real life. The arcade gives guests something to do, the bar gives them a reason to stay, and the matchmaking layer gives them a reason to interact.

If the first location proves strong unit economics, the concept can expand through additional venues, franchising/licensing, branded events and a standalone MatchPoint technology platform.
